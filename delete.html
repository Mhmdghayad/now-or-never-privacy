Delete your Now or Never account

Now or Never is published by Mohamad Ghayad. You can delete your account and all of its data at any time.

From the app

  1. Open Now or Never and go to Settings.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  3. Type the confirmation word and confirm.

This happens immediately.

Without the app

Email mohamadghayad31@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Now or Never account". The account is deleted within 30 days and you get a reply when it is done.

What is deleted

Everything: your account, profile and plan, every logged meal, weigh-ins, meal photos, subscription records and usage counts.

The one exception: if the app's administrator gave your email address free access, that address stays on the free-access list until they remove it. Ask in the same email and it is removed too.

Deleting the account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions, or it keeps renewing.
