Now or Never – Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 September 2026.

Now or Never is a nutrition diary. This page explains what the app stores and why.

What we collect

  • Account: your name, email address and a password hash, used to sign you in.
  • Plan inputs: sex, date of birth, height, weight, target weight, activity level and where you heard about the app, used to calculate your daily calorie and macro targets.
  • Diary: the meals you log (name, time, calories, macros, notes), weigh-ins, and meal photos you choose to take.

How it is used

Meal photos and descriptions are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to estimate calories and macros. They are used only to produce that estimate. Barcode and product searches go to Open Food Facts. We do not sell data and do not show ads.

Where it is stored

Data is stored in Supabase (hosted on AWS) under your own account with row-level security, so only you can read it. Photos are stored in a private bucket.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete account removes your account, diary, weigh-ins and photos immediately and permanently. You can also email the address below.
