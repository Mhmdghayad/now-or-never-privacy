Now or Never – Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 September 2026.

Now or Never is a nutrition diary. This page explains what the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and how to delete it.

What we collect

  • Account: your name and email address. If you sign in with Google, Google tells us your name, email address and account id; we never see your Google password. If you use a password, only a hash of it is stored.
  • Plan inputs: sex, date of birth, height, weight, target weight, pace, activity level, diet, allergies and where you heard about the app, used to calculate your daily calorie and nutrient targets.
  • Diary: the meals you log (name, time, calories, protein, fat, carbohydrate, sugar, fibre, notes, which meal of the day), your weigh-ins, and meal photos you choose to take or upload.
  • Voice notes: when you describe a meal by voice, your phone's speech recognition service turns it into text. We receive and keep only that text; we do not record or store your voice.
  • Subscription: if you subscribe, Google Play handles the payment. We receive the purchase token, the plan and its status and renewal date from Google, never your card details.
  • Usage counts: how many AI analyses and food searches your account made each day, used only to keep the service within its limits.

Reminders, your language, your avatar and display settings stay on your phone.

How it is used and who it is shared with

  • Anthropic (Claude API): meal photos, meal descriptions (typed or from voice), barcode label data, and - for recipe suggestions - the ingredients you enter with your diet and allergies are sent to estimate nutrition or suggest recipes. Anthropic does not use API data to train its models.
  • USDA FoodData Central: the words you search for, sent through our server.
  • Open Food Facts: barcode numbers and product searches, sent from your phone.
  • TheMealDB: the ingredients you enter in Fridge to Table, sent through our server.
  • Google: sign-in with Google and Google Play subscriptions.

A plain-text meal description (never a photo, and never linked to your account) may be kept so the same description gives the same numbers to the next person who types it. We do not sell data, do not show ads, and do not use analytics or advertising trackers.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Supabase (hosted on AWS) under your own account, with row-level security so only you can read your diary. Photos are kept in a private storage bucket. Everything is encrypted in transit.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept until you delete it. In the app, Settings → Delete account removes your account, diary, weigh-ins and photos immediately and permanently. You can also export everything first from Settings. If you cannot use the app, see how to request deletion.

Children

Now or Never is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
