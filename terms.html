What the app is

Now or Never is a nutrition diary. It estimates the calories and nutrients in the food you log, sets daily targets from the details you give it, and keeps a record of what you ate and what you weighed.

It is not medical advice

Targets, estimates and any figure the app shows are general information, not advice from a doctor or dietitian. Calorie and nutrient estimates are approximations — a photograph, a description and even a printed label are all imperfect sources, and the app tells you how confident it is for that reason. Do not use Now or Never to manage a medical condition, and speak to a qualified professional before making a significant change to how you eat. If you are pregnant, under 18, or have a history of disordered eating, please take advice before using a calorie tracker at all.
